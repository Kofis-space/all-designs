Why we exist

Booking a trip shouldn't feel like a second job.

We got tired of watching friends spend more time comparing flight aggregators than actually looking forward to the trip. Forty tabs open, three currencies, a spreadsheet nobody asked for — by the time the ticket's booked, half the excitement is gone.

Namani exists to take that part off your plate. Tell us the vibe — beach, city, wild, quiet — and we build the actual plan: flights, timing, the stuff you'd never think to ask about. You show up. That's the whole deal.
