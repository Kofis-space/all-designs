1. What we collect

When you search for a trip or request a quote, we collect: the trip details you enter (cities, dates, cabin class, traveller count), and the contact information you submit (email address and phone/WhatsApp number). We do not require an account and we do not collect payment information through this website.

2. How we use it

We use your trip details and contact information solely to prepare and send you a travel quote, follow up by email, phone, or WhatsApp, and — if you become a customer — to manage your booking. We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Where it's stored

Quote requests are sent to our internal inbox and logged in a private Google Sheet so our team can follow up quickly. These services (currently Formspree and Google Sheets) process your data only to deliver it to us — see their own privacy policies for how they handle data in transit.

4. Local browser storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your light/dark mode preference, and your session storage to temporarily hold your Travel Vibe quiz result while you complete a quote request. Nothing here is used for advertising or tracking, and it stays on your device.

5. WhatsApp

If you message us via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one. We keep WhatsApp conversations only as long as needed to assist you with your trip.

6. Advertising

We may run ads on platforms like Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google. If you arrive here from an ad, those platforms may set their own cookies or tracking pixels per their policies — we recommend reviewing Meta's and Google's advertising privacy terms directly.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@namanitravels.com.

8. Terms of use (summary)

Submitting a quote request does not obligate you to book with us, and does not guarantee pricing until confirmed by our team in writing. All bookings are subject to the individual terms and conditions of the airlines, hotels, and third-party operators involved.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@namanitravels.com or via our contact page.
